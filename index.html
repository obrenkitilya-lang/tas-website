Tax examinations

Received TRA examination findings?

A tax examination needs a structured, well-documented response within the prescribed timelines. We manage the process from the first review of TRA's findings to final resolution, while keeping management informed throughout.

Deliverables typically include a reconciliation report, the official response to TRA, and an advisory report on identified tax exposures.

Discuss your examination with us

  1. Review of TRA findings

    Analysis of the findings for each year of income, and a work plan for resolution.

  2. Collection and review of information

    Financial records, filings and supporting documents assessed against TRA's findings.

  3. Reconciliation of TRA computations

    TRA's figures compared with your records to establish the correct tax position.

  4. Response to TRA

    A comprehensive written response, prepared and submitted within the prescribed timelines.

  5. Representation and follow-up

    Handling TRA queries, meetings and correspondence until outstanding matters are resolved.

  6. Advisory on exposures

    Recommendations to management on potential exposures and corrective action.
