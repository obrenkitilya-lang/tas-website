About us

Danis Associates is a firm of Certified Public Accountants in public practice and tax consultants based in Dar es Salaam. We advise owner-managed businesses, growing companies and established organisations on tax, compliance, business registration and accounting.

Our work ranges from routine monthly filings to complex matters such as TRA tax examinations, objections and tax advisory. All services are performed in accordance with applicable tax laws and professional standards.

Our values

Qualified

Our advisers are Certified Public Accountants and tax consultants with practical experience of TRA audits and examinations, VAT refund claims, corporate income tax and financial reporting.

Clear

We agree the scope, deliverables, timeline and fee in writing before work begins, and explain your position plainly, in English or Kiswahili.

Confidential

Client records and information are used only for the engagement we have been instructed on.

Our approach

1. Initial consultation

We discuss your requirements and review the relevant documents.

2. Proposal and engagement letter

We issue a written proposal setting out the scope of work, deliverables, timeline and professional fee. Work begins once the engagement letter is signed.

3. Execution

We carry out the work and liaise with TRA, BRELA or other authorities as required.

4. Reporting

We provide progress updates, copies of everything filed, and notice of upcoming obligations.
