Business Registration & Company Secretarial

We assist local and foreign investors to establish businesses in Tanzania, and help existing companies keep their statutory records and filings up to date with the Business Registrations and Licensing Agency (BRELA).

What we do

Registration

We register companies and business names with BRELA. As part of our post-registration services, we obtain the company's Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and assist with business licences from the relevant licensing authority.

Annual returns and statutory filings

We prepare and file annual returns and ensure that the statutory filings required under the Companies Act are made on time with BRELA.

Statutory changes

We handle changes to directors, shareholding, share capital and registered office address, and file the required notifications with BRELA.

Meetings and minutes

We prepare notices, resolutions and minutes of board and shareholder meetings as required by the company.

Business licences

We prepare applications for new business licences and manage renewals before expiry.

Advice

We advise on company secretarial matters, including restructuring and changes in ownership, and keep clients informed of relevant changes in the law.
